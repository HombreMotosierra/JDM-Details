FAQ

Questions we get asked

How long does a detail take?

A Standard Detail usually takes 1 to 2 hours. Deluxe runs 2 to 3 hours, and a Premium Wash with clay bar and shampoo extraction can take 3 to 4 hours depending on the size and condition of the vehicle.

Do you need water and electricity from me?

No. We arrive fully self-contained with our own water and power. If a hookup is available and you prefer we use it, that's fine too — just let us know when you book.

What is clay bar treatment and do I need it?

Clay bar removes contaminants bonded to the paint that washing alone cannot lift — industrial fallout, rail dust, overspray. If your paint feels rough after a wash, you need it. It is included in the Premium Wash.

How long does ceramic spray protection last?

A premium ceramic spray typically lasts a few months depending on weather, parking conditions and how often the car is washed. It is not a permanent ceramic coating, but it gives you real gloss and water repellency at a fraction of the cost.

Do you take trucks, SUVs and vans?

Yes. Pricing scales with vehicle size and condition, which is why we confirm the quote before we book. Send a photo and we'll give you an exact number.

How should I prepare my car?

Just remove personal belongings and valuables from the cabin and trunk so we can reach every surface. Everything else is on us.

Ready to see your car look new again?

Message us with your vehicle and location. We'll confirm the package, the price and the time — usually the same day.
